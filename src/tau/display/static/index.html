What everything on this screen does

Status line (top middle)
What I'm doing right now: ready, listening, working out what you said, thinking (and with which model), speaking, or loading a model and how long it takes. After you change something, it says what changed.
Settings (top right)
Every setting, one section at a time: brain and model, what I watch, permissions, voice and hearing, names, pacing, display and lab. Changes take effect at once.
Connections
Everything I could connect to on your machines, grouped by place and kind. Everything starts off until you choose to switch it on, and a switch always asks before it changes anything.
Pin cards (top right)
Answers and fixes I've kept for you. It glows when one is waiting; click to read them.

Opens this help again.
What I see (left)
A panel behind the tab at the left edge. Its sections open and close: Status (every reading on the rings), Machines, Pins, and Recent actions with receipts. Click a reading for details. It stays open or closed the way you left it.
Rings
Each piece is one thing I watch, most important in the middle. Lit means fine, dim means asleep, pulsing amber means it needs a look, faint means switched off. Numbers on the rings match the list on the left; click a piece for its details card.
Details card
What a reading means, when I last checked, and buttons: Check now, Open in Settings, and Start or Stop for a service when you've allowed it.
Centre of the rings
Click it to talk, or to stop me speaking.
Triangle
My overall state. It flickers when something needs your attention.
Ticks along the top edge
One per permission ("organ"). Brighter means I'm allowed to do more. Click one to hear what it does; change them in Settings, Permissions.
Top corners
Left corner: Quiet (I stop listening and speaking). Right corner: Hard day (short, literal answers). Click to switch; they glow when on.
Bottom tip
Glows when I've kept a pin card for you. Click to open the cards.
Conversation (right)
Both sides of what we've said, with the time. Each message has a Copy button. Pin cards show inline. While I speak, the words I'm saying are marked.
Talk button
Click, then speak. I stop listening when you pause. Shortcut: Space. While I'm speaking it says Stop.
Type box and Send
Type and press Enter or click Send. The / key jumps to it.
Listen to all speech
Off unless you switch it on. It is not a wake word: when on, I treat any speech the microphone hears as talk to me. There is no working "Tau" hotword yet; use Talk or Space.
